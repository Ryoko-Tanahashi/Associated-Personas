# Task Overview
Your task is to carefully read the following phone conversation about a system inquiry from Insurance Agency A to Insurance Company B, and accurately extract the "relevant people/organizations (People)", the "issues/questions each person or organization has (Issue)", and the "solutions/answers to those issues/questions (Solution)".

In the conversation, [User] represents Agency A's representative, and [Operator] represents Company B's representative. Insurance Company B provides insurance, and customers use the insurance system managed by B.
However, because Agency A is the one that provides insurance guidance to customers, customers do not directly communicate with B.

---

# Output Targets

## People
People refers to "relevant people/organizations".
- Must be one of: "Insurance Agency Representative", "Insurance Company System Administrator", or "Customer"
- Even if the same person/organization appears multiple times in the conversation, extract them as a single person/organization.
- Even if they do not directly participate in the conversation, extract them if they are relevant to the content.
- For extraction fields, `id`, `type`, `want`, and `role` are required. Extract the others as much as possible.
- Be careful not to put issues or solutions themselves into `knowledgeLevel` or `experience`.

**Extraction Fields**
- `id`: Unique identifier for People (e.g., P1, P2, ...)
- `type`: Attribute (Customer | Insurance Agency Representative | Insurance Company System Administrator)
- `age`: Age
- `gender`: Gender
- `residence`: Living situation (e.g., lives far away, lives nearby)
- `medicalHistory`: Medical history (e.g., illness name, treatment history)
- `character`: Personality (e.g., easily flustered, efficient)
- `want`: Desire (e.g., "wants to ~", "wants to know ~"; the ultimate need behind the conversation)
- `experience`: Experience (e.g., work experience, past trouble experiences)
- `knowledgeLevel`: Knowledge level (e.g., can operate the system, does not know about ~)
- `role`: Role (the role of the person/organization that becomes clear from the conversation)

## Issue
Issue refers to "requests/challenges/questions that each person or organization has".
- Examples: "what they want to do", "what they cannot do", "what they do not understand"
- For one topic, extract one Issue for all People who have that issue.
- Each Issue must be linked to all People who hold it.
- `title` must be in a form such as "want to ~", "cannot ~", or "do not know ~".

**Extraction Fields**
- `id`: Unique identifier for Issue (e.g., I1, I2, ...)
- `title`: A concise sentence expressing the request/challenge/question
- `description`: A concrete and detailed sentence describing the request/challenge/question

## Solution
Solution refers to "solutions/answers to issues or questions".
- Examples: "what action to take", "what guidance to provide"
- Extract at most one Solution per Issue.
- Each Solution must be linked to all People who provide it.
- `title` must be in the perspective of the person facing the issue, in a form such as "do ~" or "carry out ~".

**Extraction Fields**
- `id`: Unique identifier for Solution (e.g., S1, S2, ...)
- `title`: A concise sentence expressing the solution/answer
- `description`: A concrete and detailed sentence describing the solution/answer

---
## Relations
### 1. Relationships between People and Issue/Solution
- `Issue → People: isHeldBy`
    - Indicates the relationship to People who hold a given Issue.
    - Example: An issue regarding enrollment in disability income insurance held by a customer.
- `Solution → People: isHeldBy`
    - Indicates the relationship to People who provide a given Solution.
    - Example: A login issue solution provided by an insurance company system administrator.

### 2. Relationships between Issues
- `Issue A → Issue B: isGeneratedBy`
    - Indicates that Issue A is generated by Issue B.
    - Example: A customer's question generates an issue for the insurance agency representative.

### 3. Relationships between Issue and Solution
- `Issue → Solution: isResolvedBy`
    - Indicates that an Issue is resolved by a specific Solution.
    - Example: A login issue for an insurance agency representative is resolved by password reset guidance.
- `Issue → Solution: isGeneratedBy`
    - Indicates that a new Issue is generated by providing a Solution.
    - Example: Guidance on how to order pamphlets generates a login issue.

---

# Extraction Rules
- Use **only information inferable from the call**. Use of general/common knowledge is prohibited.

# Notes
- Output must be in JSON format only. Output in any non-JSON format is prohibited.
- The output JSON must strictly follow the structure above. Missing or extra fields are prohibited.
- Ensure that each People node is linked to **at least one Issue or Solution**.
- Not all Issues are required to have a linked Solution.
- **Do not generate relationships that cannot be read from the conversation.**
- Strictly preserve the direction of `isHeldBy`, `isGeneratedBy`, and `isResolvedBy`.
- Do not include masked information such as [LOCATION], [ORGANIZATION], [PERSON], etc., in extracted information.
