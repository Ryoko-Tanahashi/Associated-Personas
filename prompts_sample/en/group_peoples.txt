The following contains information about people related to a common issue or solution.
Please extract all shared characteristics from these people and create a GroupedPeople node.

**Important instructions:**
- Extract only characteristics that are common among the given People.
- Do not include items that are not shared.
- Avoid adding information based on assumptions.
- Describe characteristics as ranges or tendencies, not as specific concrete values.
- If there is no common trend and the data is too diverse, do not include that item.

**GroupedPeople fields**
- `id`: A unique identifier for the GroupedPeople (e.g., GP1, GP2, ...)
- `type`: Attribute (customer | insurance agency representative | insurance company system administrator)
- `age`: Age
- `residence`: Living situation (e.g., living far away, living nearby)
- `medicalHistory`: Medical history (e.g., illnesses, treatment history)
- `character`: Personality traits (e.g., easily panicked, efficient)
- `want`: Desire (e.g., "wants to ...", "wants to know ..."; the underlying need they ultimately want to satisfy)
- `experience`: Experience (e.g., work experience, past trouble incidents)
- `knowledgeLevel`: Knowledge level (e.g., can operate systems, does not know about ...)
- `role`: Role (the role of the person/organization revealed through the interaction)
- `source_ids`: List of source People IDs and CALL_IDs

# Output format

    ]
  }
}
